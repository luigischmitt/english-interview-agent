## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a calm rehearsal desk that makes the next useful practice action obvious; it refuses the category-default marketing hero, dashboard metrics, and competing calls to action.

OWN-WORLD: Warm bone and ink surfaces, sparse hairline rules, precise teal as the single action accent, and editorial utility typography. Navigation stays legible at every width; controls use restrained daisyUI semantics, modest radii, and visible focus instead of chrome or decoration.

STORY: A visitor understands the audience and promise in one scan, creates a practice space or signs in, then learns the simple practice loop. An authenticated candidate sees what to do now, may warm up in place, starts one real session, or hands off to Progress for history.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On public Home, a compact masthead leads into a left-weighted H1 and short supporting line, with one primary Create a practice space action and quiet Sign in beside it; a short three-step How it works sequence anchors the lower fold. On authenticated Home, the same masthead leads to one Start practice action, a small warm-up prompt, a slim context rail, and a clearly separated View progress handoff.

FORM: Code-led editorial operating surface, selected from the approved ENG-28 direction (seed/form reference: brief-eng-28-approved); no concept tournament or image assets for this run. Server page selects public versus authenticated shell; client shell preserves interview room and Progress behavior.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
